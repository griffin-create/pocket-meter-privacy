Pocket Meter & Polaroid Scanner: Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email griffinmalone2@gmail.com and I'll get back to you. You can also send an idea from inside the app: More options → What would you like to see?

The light meter reads differently from my camera

Phone cameras vary. Meter the same scene with your camera or a trusted meter, then use More options → Adjust → Calibrate this phone to match. It's saved for every reading.

The scanner didn't find my print

Pick the right print type and border colour on the Scan tab, lay the print on a plain surface (dark for white borders, light for black borders) and get the whole print in the photo. If it still misses, open the scan and use Adjust to drag the corners.

My scan has glare

Use Glare-free scan on the Scan tab: take 2–3 photos, tilting the phone a little each time.

Restoring Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase. On a new phone, open Pro and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Privacy

Your photos stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
