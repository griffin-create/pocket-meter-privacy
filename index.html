Pocket Meter & Polaroid Scanner: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

Pocket Meter & Polaroid Scanner ("the app") is made by Griffin Malone. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: your photos stay on your phone, and the app doesn't track you.

Camera and photos

The light meter uses your camera to measure light. Meter readings are worked out on your phone, and no images are saved or sent anywhere.

The scanner uses photos you choose or take to frame your prints. All processing happens on your phone. Framed scans are stored in the app on your device, and they're only shared or saved when you tap Share or Save.

Feedback ("What would you like to see?")

If you send an idea, the app sends what you typed, the email address if you choose to add one, and the app version and Android version to a Google Form owned by the developer. This is only used to read and reply to feedback. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Forms.

Purchases

Pocket Meter Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details. Purchases are managed with RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status so Pro can be unlocked and restored. RevenueCat's privacy policy applies.

App updates

The app checks Expo's servers for updates. That request includes technical details like the app version and platform, which servers need in order to respond. It contains no personal information.

What the app doesn't do

  • No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • No account or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts or microphone access.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting a scan in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes your scans and settings from your device. To have a feedback message you sent deleted, email the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
